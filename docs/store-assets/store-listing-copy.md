# EkamCore — Store Listing Copy (v1.0 soft launch)

_Date: 2026-06-13. **Scope discipline:** this copy describes **what the app does today (Phase 0)** —
private on-device AI chat, a curated model catalog, vision/image understanding, fully offline. It is
voiced in the long-term **"Own your intelligence"** brand but does **not** advertise unbuilt Phase 1
features (life-memory recall) — both stores reject copy describing features not in the build, and it
would burn the trust the product is built on. Update this when Phase 1 ships._

The genuine aha that is **true today**: *a real AI that runs entirely on your phone — private, free,
no account, works offline.* Lead with that.

## Full description (Google Play — ≤4000 chars; also use for App Store description)

```
Own your intelligence.

EkamCore is a real AI assistant that runs entirely on your phone — no cloud, no
account, no sign-up. Your conversations never leave your device.

Most AI apps send everything you type to someone else's servers. EkamCore doesn't.
You download an AI model once, and from then on it runs fully on your device — even
in airplane mode. Private by design, not by promise.

WHY EKAMCORE
• Truly private — your chats stay on your device. We can't read them.
• Works offline — once a model is downloaded, no internet required.
• No account, no subscription, no ads, no tracking.
• Free.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Chat with capable open AI models, right on your phone.
• Choose from a curated catalog of models — from featherweight to powerful — with
  size and speed estimates tailored to YOUR device.
• Understand images — pick a vision-capable model and ask about a photo.
• Browse and download more models when you want to.

BUILT FOR OWNERSHIP
EkamCore is part of a bigger idea: your intelligence should belong to you — running
on hardware you own, with data that never leaves your control. This is just the
beginning.

Download a model. Go offline. Own your intelligence.

—
Note: AI models are downloaded over the internet (large files; Wi-Fi recommended).
After that, chatting works fully offline and your conversations stay on your device.
```

_(~1,150 chars — well within both limits. The closing "Note" keeps us honest about the one-time
model download, which is also good App Store review hygiene.)_
